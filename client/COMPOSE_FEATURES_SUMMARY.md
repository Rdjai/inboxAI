## 🎮 How to Use

### Auto-Save
Just start typing - your draft is automatically saved every 3 seconds. The "Saved" timestamp appears in the header.

### Quick Templates
Click any template button to insert pre-written text at your cursor position:
- 👋 **Greeting**: Professional opening
- 📝 **Closing**: Professional sign-off
- ⚡ **Follow-up**: Follow-up message
- 📅 **Meeting**: Meeting request
- ✨ **Thank You**: Gratitude expression

### Keyboard Shortcuts
- **Ctrl+Enter** (Cmd+Enter on Mac): Send email
- **Ctrl+S** (Cmd+S on Mac): Save draft
- **Esc**: Cancel/Close
- **?**: Show help guide

### Email Scheduling
1. Click the "Schedule" button
2. Select date and time
3. Click "Schedule Email" to queue

### Priority Levels
Select from dropdown:
- **Low**: Non-urgent
- **Normal**: Standard (default)
- **High**: Important
- **Urgent**: Critical

### Tone Selector
1. Write your message
2. Select desired tone
3. Click "Apply Tone"
4. Review and edit as needed
